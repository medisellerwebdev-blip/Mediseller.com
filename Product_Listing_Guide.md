# Mediseller 2.0 - Product Listing Guide (Hindi)

Yeh guide un sabhi logon ke liye hai jo Mediseller website par naye products add karna chahte hain ya existing products ko edit karna chahte hain. Website par product list karna bahut aasan hai. Niche har ek section ko detail mein simple language mein samjhaya gaya hai.

## 1. Main Info (Product ki Basic Jaankari)
* **Product Name:** Yahan product ka main naam daalein (Jaise: Gleevec).
* **URL Slug (Custom URL):** Yeh website ka link banata hai. Aap **"Generate from Name"** button par click karke isse automatically bana sakte hain. Yeh SEO ke liye bahut zaroori hai. Ya Manually bhi aap type kar sakte (Jaise: Sovihep-v-400).
* **Generic Name:** Dawa ka formula ya generic naam daalein (Jaise: Imatinib Mesylate).
* **Brand Name:** Kis brand ki dawa hai (Jaise: Novartis).
* **Manufacturer:** Dawa banane wali company ka pura naam (Jaise: Novartis India Ltd).
* **Category:** Dropdown se dawa ki sahi category select karein (Jaise: Cancer, HIV/AIDS, etc.).

## 2. Details (Dawa ki detail)
* **Dosage:** Dawa ka power kya hai (Jaise: 400mg).
* **Form:** Dawa kis form mein hai (Jaise: Tablet, Capsule, Injection).
* **Quantity per Pack:** Ek pack mein kitni goliyan/quantity hai (Jaise: 30).

## 3. Pricing (Price & Discounts)
* **Original Price ($):** Dollar mein product ka asli MRP daalein.
* **Sale Price ($):** Dollar mein woh price daalein jis par aap bechna chahte hain.
* **Original Price (₹):** Rupees mein product ka asli MRP.
* **Sale Price (₹):** Rupees mein sale price.
* **Discount (%):** Discount automatically calculate ho jata hai ya aap khud bhi type kar sakte hain.

## 4. Inventory & Prescription (Stock aur Parchi)
* **Requires Prescription:** Agar dawa ke liye doctor ki parchi (prescription) zaroori hai, toh is button ko ON (Blue) rakhein.
* **In Stock:** Agar dawa available hai, toh isse ON (Green) rakhein. Out of stock hone par OFF kar dein.

## 5. Description (Dawa ke baare mein)
* **Product Description:** Yahan dawa ke baare mein detail mein likhein ki dawa kis kaam aati hai. (Aap bold, italic, list jaisi formatting ka use kar sakte hain).

## 6. Main Image (Main Photo)
* **Upload Image from Computer:** Is option par click karke apne computer se product ki photo upload karein.
* **Direct Image URL:** Agar photo ka link hai toh aap direct link bhi paste kar sakte hain.

## 7. Usage & Safety (Istemal aur Suraksha)
* **Usage Instructions:** Dawa kaise leni hai, uski instructions likhein. (Dhyaan rahe ki har ek instruction ek nayi line mein likhein).
* **Side Effects:** Dawa ke side effects kya ho sakte hain, woh yahan likhein.
* **Storage Info:** Dawa ko kahan aur kaise store karna hai (Jaise: Store at room temperature).

## 8. Social Proof (Customer Trust ke liye)
* **Manual Rating (1-5):** Product ko kitni star rating deni hai (Jaise: 4.8).
* **Orders Count:** Dikhane ke liye ki yeh dawa kitni baar bik chuki hai (Jaise: 100).

## 9. Gallery (Extra Photos)
* **Product Image Gallery:** Customers ko bharosa dilane ke liye kam se kam 3 photos upload karne ki salah di jati hai. Aap "Add More Images" par click karke aur photos ke link/URL daal sakte hain.

## 10. SEO & Search Optimization (Google Ranking)
Yeh section Google mein product ko rank karne ke liye hai:
* **Auto-Generate Tags & Slug:** Is button par click karne se Google search (SEO) ke liye Title, Description aur Keywords apne aap fill ho jayenge. Yeh bahut useful aur time-saving feature hai!
* **Meta Title, Meta Description, Keywords:** Aap inhein manually bhi edit kar sakte hain agar zaroorat lage.

## 11. Save Options (Kaam Save Karna)
Jab sab details fill ho jayein toh niche 3 options milenge:
* **Discard Changes:** Agar changes cancel karne hain toh ispar click karein.
* **Save & Continue:** Product save karke page par hi rehne ke liye (agar kuch aur add karna baaki ho).
* **Create Product / Update Product:** Final save karne aur form ko close karne ke liye ispar click karein.
